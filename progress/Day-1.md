## Content:
Today is mainly about understand how to use Cisco Packet Tracer, and understand Cisco Certifications system. 

  Things learned: 
  
    - Online CCNA could be taken at home, but I don't think that is guaranteed. There are places around HCMC and HNC to take it offline.

    - Fee is $300
    - There are 3 main cert for network engineer: CCNA, CCNP and CCIE
    - You can take free practices online if you dig hard enough
